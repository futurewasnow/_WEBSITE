Get your free Digital Presence Report

We audit how your property shows up online and compare it with similar places nearby. Free, no obligation, delivered on WhatsApp within 2 business days.

What we check

  • Booking sites. How your listing reads on the platforms guests compare you on.
  • Your website. Whether it loads well on a phone and answers the questions guests ask before they book.
  • Direct booking. How easy it is to reserve with you instead of through a portal.
  • Photos. Coverage of rooms, common areas and the setting.
  • 360° and Street View. Whether guests can look around before they decide.
  • Google profile. Completeness, photos, reviews and how you reply to them.

How it works

  1. Send the basics. Your property name and a Google Maps link or website.
  2. We audit and compare. Each point above is checked and set beside similar places nearby. The comparison stays anonymous: no other property is named.
  3. You get the report on WhatsApp. Within 2 business days, in English or Spanish, with what is working and what to fix first.
