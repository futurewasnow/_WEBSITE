Why Tamarindo businesses use virtual tours

Tamarindo sells to two audiences at once, and they want different things from the same property. Holiday guests are choosing a week; buyers and long-stay renters are choosing somewhere to live. Both are usually deciding from another country, and both are trying to answer a question a photo gallery is bad at: how close is this actually to the beach?

Proximity is the whole Tamarindo market, and it is the single hardest thing to convey in stills. A continuous walkthrough that goes from the room to the door to the street to the sand settles it in about fifteen seconds.

Working in Tamarindo

Guanacaste light is the defining technical problem on this coast. The dry season delivers extremely strong, high-contrast sun, and the classic beachfront shot — a shaded interior with a blazing bright doorway or window opening onto sand — is precisely the frame that an auto-stitching consumer 360° camera cannot hold.

We shoot bracketed and stitch by hand, which keeps both the interior and the view outside it properly exposed in the same scene. Exterior and drone work is scheduled around the ends of the day, when the light does the property a favour rather than flattening it.

About Tamarindo, Guanacaste

Tamarindo sits on Guanacaste’s northern Pacific coast and functions as the commercial centre of the surrounding beach communities. Nearby:

  • Beaches: Playa Tamarindo and Playa Langosta, with Playa Grande across the estuary
  • Protected areas: Las Baulas National Marine Park, a nesting site for leatherback turtles
  • Access: Liberia’s international airport, which puts the town within a straightforward drive of an arriving flight
  • Market: A dense concentration of hotels, restaurants, surf schools and property agencies in a small area

That density is the reason Tamarindo businesses need to differentiate. In a town where a visitor can compare a dozen similar-looking beachfront listings in an evening, being the one they can actually walk through is a meaningful advantage. Tamarindo is part of our wider Guanacaste coverage.
