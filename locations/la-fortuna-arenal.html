About La Fortuna & Arenal

La Fortuna de San Carlos is a vibrant town nestled at the base of Arenal Volcano, one of Costa Rica's most recognizable landmarks. The region is renowned for:

  • Natural Hot Springs: Tabacón, Baldi, Ecotermales, and many more
  • Adventure Activities: Zip-lining, canyoning, white water rafting, ATV tours
  • Wildlife: Arenal Oasis, rainforest hikes, bird watching
  • Luxury Resorts: World-class accommodations with volcano views

As one of Costa Rica's top tourist destinations, La Fortuna businesses compete for attention from travelers worldwide. A professional 360° virtual tour gives your property a competitive edge, allowing potential guests to experience your unique offerings before they book.
