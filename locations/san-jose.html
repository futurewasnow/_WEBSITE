Why San José businesses use virtual tours

San José is the one part of Costa Rica where the customer is usually already nearby. Someone searching for a restaurant in Barrio Escalante, a clinic in Escazú or a co-working space in Santa Ana is making a decision about somewhere they could reach in twenty minutes — and they are almost always making it on a phone, inside Google.

That changes what a virtual tour is for. In a beach town the tour sells a place someone has never been to. In the capital it does something more immediate: it answers “what is this place actually like inside?” before someone commits to the drive, the parking and the trip across town.

Working in San José

The Central Valley is straightforward to shoot and awkward to schedule. Traffic dictates everything: we plan interior captures around the hours when a space is genuinely clear, which for restaurants usually means well before service and for offices means early morning or the weekend.

Light is the other constraint. Valley interiors tend to combine deep floor plates with very bright windows, which is exactly the situation that defeats an auto-stitching 360° camera. Every scene is captured as a bracketed exposure set and hand-stitched, so the room and the view out of it are both correctly exposed.

About San José & the Central Valley

San José sits at roughly 1,170 metres in the Central Valley and, with its surrounding cantons, holds the majority of the country’s business activity. For a commercial property the region is defined by:

  • Business districts: Escazú, Santa Ana and Sabana — corporate offices, free-trade-zone tenants and international headquarters
  • Culture: Teatro Nacional, Museo del Oro Precolombino, Museo Nacional and the Jade museum
  • Dining: Barrio Escalante’s Paseo Gastronómico, plus the Mercado Central and the Barrio Amon district
  • Access: The main international gateway at Juan Santamaría, and the road links to both coasts

Because the capital is where most Costa Rican businesses have a physical address, it is also where a Google Business Profile does the most work. Publishing a verified 360° interior through the Street View Trusted programme puts the inside of the premises directly into Maps and Search results.
