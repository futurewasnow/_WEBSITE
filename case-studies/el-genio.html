Case study

El Genio

A complete property showcase with interactive navigation and information hotspots throughout.

Costa Rica Property showcase 360° virtual tour
The brief

What this property needed

El Genio was captured as a complete property showcase — the whole space, walkable end to end, rather than a selection of highlights. That framing matters when the audience is deciding remotely: a highlight reel invites the question of what was left out, whereas a continuous walkthrough answers it.

Approach

How we captured it

We photographed across the property and built interactive navigation between the spaces, with information hotspots throughout. Every scene is 12K HDR and hand-stitched. The tour is structured so a visitor can move through the property in a natural order, rather than jumping between disconnected viewpoints.

Scope

What we delivered

  • 360° photography across the property
  • Interactive navigation between spaces
  • Information hotspots throughout
  • Tour link ready for listings and sharing
Why it matters

The problem this solves

A property that can be walked end to end online reaches buyers and guests who are deciding before they ever travel.

Walk through the live tour
In use

How the tour gets used

The finished tour is delivered as a link ready for property listings and for direct sharing, which suits a property being marketed largely to people who are not currently in the country. Someone can complete a full walkthrough before deciding whether the property is worth a trip, which filters the enquiries in a useful direction.

There is a second, quieter use for a complete walkthrough: it settles questions before they become objections. Distance, layout, how rooms relate to one another, what the light is like at different points in the property — these are the things people ask about repeatedly, and they are all faster to show than to describe. A tour that covers the property end to end answers most of them without anyone having to reply.

Common questions

What was captured at El Genio?

The property in full, with interactive navigation between spaces and information hotspots throughout, delivered as a shareable tour link.

Is the tour suitable for property listings?

Yes. It is delivered as a link that can be attached to listings or sent directly to interested parties.

What resolution are the panoramas?

All scenes are captured at 12K, HDR-blended and hand-stitched rather than auto-stitched in camera.
