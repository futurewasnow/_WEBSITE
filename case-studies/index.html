How to read these

What each case study covers

Each page follows the same shape, so you can compare projects rather than read marketing. The brief explains what that property actually needed and why a gallery was not going to do it — a resort selling multi-night stays sight-unseen has a different problem from a restaurant competing inside Google Maps.

How we captured it covers the technical decisions: how many scenes, whether drone aerials were involved, and how the hardest shot on the property was handled. On almost every one of these, that shot is a shaded interior opening onto bright Costa Rican sun, which is exactly where consumer 360° cameras fail.

How the tour gets used is the part most portfolios leave out. A tour that sits in a gallery nobody visits is worth very little; one embedded on a booking page, attached to a Google Business Profile, and sent as a link when an enquiry arrives is doing three jobs at once.

What these pages deliberately do not contain is invented performance figures. Where a client has shared real numbers we will publish them; until then, the tours themselves are the evidence — open any of them and judge the work directly.
