Common questions

Why hire you for traditional media instead of just 360°?

Because we shoot 360° we obsess over light in every direction — that obsession transfers to your editorial photo and video work. Plus you get one team that understands your brand across all media.

Can you do both 360° and traditional in one shoot?

Yes — and we encourage it. Stack the sessions so you get a 360° tour AND social content from the same day's setup. Best value.

Do you handle drone for traditional video too?

Yes — same MICITT-licensed pilots, same cinematic eye.

Can you produce content for paid ads?

Yes — we deliver in the dimensions and durations Meta, Google, and TikTok require. We also understand the creative principles each platform's algorithm rewards.

How long does a typical shoot day produce?

A full day: ~30 hero stills, 5-10 lifestyle scenes, 1 brand video (2 min) + 3-5 social cutdowns.
