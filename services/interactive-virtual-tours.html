Expert Insights

What are interactive hotspots in a virtual tour?

Hotspots are clickable areas within the 360° view that can trigger text, images, videos, audio, or links to external websites like booking engines.

Can virtual tours be white-labeled for my brand?

Yes, we can customize the user interface, add your logo, and use your brand colors to ensure the tour feels like an integrated part of your website.

Do you provide hosting for the virtual tours?

We offer secure, high-speed hosting for all our interactive tours, or we can provide the files for you to host on your own server.

Common questions

Can the tour have multiple floors / buildings?

Yes — unlimited scenes, linked via floor plans, maps, or scene-selector menus. We've built tours with 80+ scenes.

How do visitors navigate between scenes?

Three ways: click hotspot arrows (most intuitive), tap a thumbnail in the scene-selector, or click a location on the embedded floor plan. They choose what feels natural.

Can I update the tour after delivery?

Yes — we offer a self-service editor where you can swap text, photos, and hotspot links yourself. For new scenes you'd schedule a re-shoot.

How fast does the tour load?

First scene loads in 1-3 seconds even on slow 3G. Subsequent scenes pre-fetch in the background so transitions are instant.

Does this work in VR headsets?

Yes — Meta Quest, Vision Pro, and any WebXR-compatible headset can load the tour directly from your URL. No app install needed.
