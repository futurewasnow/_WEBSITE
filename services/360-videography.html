Common Questions

Expert Insights

Where can 360 video be played?

360° videos can be uploaded to YouTube, Facebook, and specialized VR headsets. They can also be embedded directly on your website for browser-based viewing.

What is the difference between 360 photo and 360 video?

A 360° photo is a static panoramic view, while a 360° video is a moving spherical capture that allows the viewer to look around while the action unfolds.

How do you handle audio in 360 video?

We can record spatial (ambisonic) audio, which changes direction as the viewer turns their head, creating a deeply realistic sense of presence.

Common questions

Is 360° video the same as VR?

Not exactly. 360° video lets you look around but the viewpoint is fixed. True VR allows movement. For most marketing use cases (hotel walkthroughs, adventure highlights), 360° video gives 95% of the wow at 30% of the cost.

How long should my 360° video be?

60–90 seconds for marketing. 3-5 minutes for an immersive guided tour. Longer than that and viewers lose attention.

Do you provide voiceover or narration?

We work with bilingual (EN/ES) voiceover artists and can script the narration based on your goals. Add it to your project for $300–$600.

Can the video play on regular YouTube?

Yes. YouTube supports 360° playback natively — viewers can drag with their mouse or move their phone to look around. Same goes for Facebook and Vimeo.

How much storage / bandwidth does it use?

Final files are 200MB–2GB depending on length. We host them on a global CDN so your visitors stream smoothly anywhere.
