Common questions

Which phones can use WebAR?

Nearly all phones made since 2018: iPhone 6S and newer (iOS 11+), most Android phones with ARCore (Android 7+). No special hardware needed.

How is this different from VR?

VR is a separate world (you wear a headset). AR puts digital content INTO the real world via your phone camera. AR has 100× more reach because everyone has a phone.

Do I need to provide 3D models?

We can build them from photos (photogrammetry), source from your existing brand assets, or design from scratch. Cost varies based on complexity.

Can the AR be triggered by a real object?

Yes — image targets (a poster, business card, product label) or location-based (GPS). Or simply via QR/link.

How long do AR experiences take to build?

Simple product placement: 1-2 weeks. Complex multi-scene AR with custom 3D: 4-8 weeks.
