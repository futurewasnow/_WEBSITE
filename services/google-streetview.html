Common questions

Is this the same as the Street View I see on Google Maps?

Yes — the exact same product. The difference is your business gets an interior 360° walkthrough that visitors can explore from your Google Business Profile, just like the official Google car imagery outside.

How long does it stay on Google?

Permanently, as long as your Google Business Profile is active. We can also update it any time your space changes.

Will this help my Google ranking?

Indirectly, yes. Businesses with rich Google profiles (photos, Street View, reviews) rank higher in local search and the Google Maps pack. Street View signals 'verified, real, active business' to Google.

Can I see the imagery before it goes live?

Yes. We deliver a preview link first; you approve, then we publish to Google Maps.

Does this work for outdoor venues?

Absolutely — beaches, gardens, adventure parks, even waterfalls. We've done Street View for hiking trails.
