Common questions

How is your 12K HDR different from standard 360° photography?

Standard 360° cameras capture at 4K-6K resolution and auto-stitch in-camera. We shoot multiple bracketed exposures at 12K per panorama and hand-stitch in post. The result has 4–9× the pixel density and properly exposed interiors AND windows (the hardest shot in real estate photography).

How long does the shoot take?

Most properties: 2-4 hours on-site. A 12-bedroom resort: half a day. We work fast but never rush — every scene gets the lighting setup it deserves.

What do I need to do before the shoot?

Clean and stage like you would for a magazine cover. Hide cleaning supplies, fluff pillows, turn on every light, and let us know about any timing constraints (sunset, busy hours, etc.). We'll send you a one-page prep checklist.

Can I embed the tour on my website?

Yes. We provide an iframe embed code that works on any site — Squarespace, Webflow, WordPress, custom HTML. We can also publish directly to Google Street View if you want SEO benefits.

What if I need to add more scenes later?

Easy. We keep your project files for 2 years. Adding scenes is a half-day re-shoot at our standard rate.
