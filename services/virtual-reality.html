Common questions

Who actually has a VR headset?

Mostly: real estate developers, architects, tech early adopters, and trade-show buyers. The hardware is still niche but growing 30%/year. If your buyers fly in for sales meetings, having VR available is a serious differentiator.

How is VR different from 360° photography?

360° photography is one viewpoint per scene; VR is a full 3D scene you can walk around in. VR requires more capture (photogrammetry or LiDAR scanning) but creates a 10× more immersive experience.

Do I need to buy headsets?

Only if you want to demo at events. Otherwise visitors with their own headset access via a URL.

Can the VR experience also work in browser?

Yes — WebXR support means the same content runs in a headset OR a phone's gyroscope view OR a desktop browser with mouse drag.

How long does a VR project take?

Hybrid 360°+stereoscopic real-estate tour: 3-4 weeks. Full custom 3D-scanned VR application: 8-16 weeks.
