What is 360 drone photography?

It's an aerial panorama that allows users to look in every direction—up, down, and all around—from a bird's-eye view, providing a complete sense of location.

Are your drone pilots licensed in Costa Rica?

We review the proposed flight, applicable permissions and operator documentation before confirming aerial capture.
