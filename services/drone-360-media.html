Common questions

Is it legal to fly drones in Costa Rica?

Yes, with proper licensing from MICITT and respect for national park rules. We carry both. Some areas (around airports, presidential residence, certain reserves) require special permits — we handle the paperwork.

Can you fly inside national parks?

Most national parks prohibit drone flights without a specific research/commercial permit. We can apply on your behalf — typical lead time is 2-4 weeks.

Do drones disturb wildlife?

We use only the quietest drones (Mavic 3 series — ~75dB at altitude) and never fly close to nesting sites or animal aggregations. Wildlife safety is part of our flight planning.

How high can you fly?

Up to 120 meters AGL (legal limit in Costa Rica). For most architectural shots we stay at 30-80m for the best perspective.

What if the weather is bad?

We watch forecasts and reschedule if conditions are unsafe (high wind, rain). Rescheduling is free.
