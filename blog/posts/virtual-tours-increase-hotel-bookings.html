What Do Guests See Before They Book Your Costa Rica Hotel?

A checklist of what guests check online before they book, where a 360° tour fits, and a practical way to measure what it brings.

360° photography by YouSee360. Full tours: Junglo Resort & Spa · Casa del Río · Casa del Río — pool

Before a guest books a hotel in Costa Rica, they usually look at the same handful of things: the booking sites, your own website, your photos, whether they can look around in 360°, and your Google profile. If any of those is thin or out of date, the guest moves on without asking. This checklist walks through each one, shows where a virtual tour fits, and uses a real resort project as the example.

A hotel virtual tour helps guests inspect rooms and shared spaces before making an enquiry. Its value depends on the questions it answers and where it appears in the booking journey. A tour alone does not guarantee more reservations.

Show the spaces guests need to compare

Start with the differences between room categories: the view, layout, terrace and access to shared areas. Show the route to the pool or wellness spaces where that is useful. Keep ordinary photographs alongside the tour so a guest can get a quick overview or explore in depth.

A real example: Junglo Resort & Spa

Our Junglo project in La Fortuna connects guest and common areas into an interactive walkthrough, with information hotspots and an embed for the property website. Explore the actual tour to see the navigation and captured spaces. We are showing the delivered work, not claiming an independently measured booking uplift.

Put the tour beside a useful next step

  • On a room page, place the tour beside the room description and availability button.
  • In a reservations reply, link directly to the space the guest asked about.
  • On your own website, embed the tour with a clear title and a link to open it separately.
  • For third-party listings, check the platform's current rules for external links and embedded content.

Measure bookings, not just time spent looking

Record tour opens, enquiry submissions, booking-engine clicks and confirmed direct bookings separately. Compare similar periods and account for room prices, advertising, availability and seasonality. If you can run an A/B test with enough traffic, compare booking completion for equivalent groups.

For a useful case study, publish the dates, sample size, baseline, intervention and measurement method with the property's permission. Longer visits alone do not establish additional bookings, and an enquiry is not a completed sale.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do virtual tours increase hotel bookings?

A virtual tour can help guests understand a property before booking. Measure completed bookings against a comparable baseline; results depend on placement, traffic, availability and pricing.

Are 360 tours worth the investment for resorts?

Compare the quoted cost with the value of the guest questions it answers and the bookings you can measure. A useful tour is not a guarantee of a particular return or payback period.

How do I add a virtual tour to my hotel website?

Virtual tours can be easily embedded on any website using an iframe, just like a YouTube video. It requires no special software for your guests to view on their phones or computers.
