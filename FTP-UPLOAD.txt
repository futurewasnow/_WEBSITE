ORDER MATTERS. Upload in this order so no page is ever live while
pointing at a stylesheet that has not arrived yet:

  STEP 1 - assets first:  css/  js/  images/  videos/  fonts/
  STEP 2 - then the pages: all .html files
  STEP 3 - then:           .htaccess  sitemap.xml  robots.txt  manifest.webmanifest
  STEP 4 - then delete the files listed at the bottom

WATCH OUT: .htaccess starts with a dot. Most FTP clients hide dotfiles
by default - turn on "show hidden files" or it will not upload. Without
it you lose the HTTPS redirect, the WebP serving, caching and security
headers.

WATCH OUT: the CSS filenames are content-hashed (css/bundle-<hash>.css).
The new HTML points at new hashes. If you upload HTML without the new
css/ files, every page loads unstyled.
