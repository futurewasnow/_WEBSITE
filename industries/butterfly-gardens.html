The Challenge

Capturing Delicate Detail

Butterfly gardens are full of movement and macro-level details that traditional wide-angle photography often fails to highlight.

  • Subject Movement: Capturing clear 360° images in an environment full of flying subjects.
  • Scale Disparity: Showing the beauty of the garden while also highlighting small chrysalises and eggs.

FAQ

Common Questions

How do you capture moving butterflies?

We use ultra-fast shutter speeds and multi-shot blending to ensure that moving subjects don't cause ghosting in the final panorama.

Can we show different life stages?

Yes. We can include informational "pop-up" macro photos that show eggs, caterpillars, and chrysalises at specific locations in the tour.

Common Questions

Expert Insights

How can 360 tours help a butterfly garden?

Virtual tours allow potential visitors to preview the beauty and scale of your sanctuary, making it a 'must-visit' destination in their Costa Rica itinerary.

Can we identify species within the virtual tour?

Yes, we can add interactive 'species tags' as hotspots that display names and facts about the butterflies when a user clicks on them.
