Common Questions

Expert Insights

Can you create educational hotspots in museum tours?

Yes, we can embed text, audio narrations, and close-up macro photos into the 360° environment to turn a tour into an educational experience.

How high is the resolution for artwork?

We use specialized high-dynamic-range photography to ensure colors are accurate and fine details of paintings or artifacts are preserved in the digital twin.

Can virtual tours be used for fundraising?

Absolutely. Many museums use virtual tours as a benefit for members or as a way to attract international donors and sponsors.
