The Challenge

Balancing Access & Preservation

National parks face the dual challenge of increasing visitor engagement while protecting delicate ecosystems from over-tourism.

  • Physical Barriers: Many stunning locations are inaccessible to those with limited mobility.
  • Environmental Stress: High physical traffic can damage trails and disturb local wildlife.

FAQ

Common Questions

How do you handle weather patterns in the rainforest?

We monitor weather patterns closely. For nature photography, we wait for the optimal light conditions—often "bright overcast" for forest floor details or "blue sky" for vistas.

Are these tours helpful for educational purposes?

Yes. We can integrate informational hotspots, audio narration, and interactive maps that allow students to learn about specific species and geological features.

Do you offer Google Maps integration for park trails?

Yes, we can publish trail walkthroughs directly to Google Maps, improving accessibility and allowing users to "hike" the park virtually from anywhere.

Common Questions

Expert Insights

Are virtual tours used for park education?

Yes, they allow students and researchers worldwide to explore unique Costa Rican ecosystems and learn about biodiversity without disturbing the habitat.

Can we use 360 tours for accessible tourism?

Absolutely. Virtual tours provide a way for people with mobility challenges to experience trails and viewpoints that might otherwise be inaccessible.
