FAQ

Common Questions

How long does the photography process take?

For a typical 100-room resort, we can complete all photography and aerials in 3-4 days. We work around your occupancy schedule to minimize disruption to guests.

Can these tours be embedded on my website?

Absolutely. We provide a simple embed code (iframe) that works with WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, and custom HTML sites. It loads instantly and doesn't slow down your page.

Do you offer Google Street View integration?

Yes, we are Google Trusted Photographers. We can publish your virtual tour directly to Google Maps, improving your local SEO and visibility.

What happens if the weather is bad?

We monitor weather patterns closely. For aerials and exteriors, we only shoot on clear "blue sky" days. We build flexible contingency days into our schedule.
