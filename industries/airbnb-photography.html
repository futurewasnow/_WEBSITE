Why it matters

In Costa Rica, the photography is the viewing

Most people who book a villa here, or buy one, never see it first. They are in Denver or Toronto or Frankfurt, deciding between a handful of properties on a screen, and the photography is doing the entire job that a walk-through would do anywhere else. It has to carry the rooms, the light, the setting and the distance to the water, because nothing else in the listing can.

That is also why the failure modes cost so much here. Interiors that go yellow under warm bulbs. Windows blown to white, so the ocean view you are actually selling simply disappears. Wide lenses bowing the walls of a room that is genuinely beautiful. A midday exterior with hard shadows across a facade that would have looked extraordinary forty minutes later. None of that is taste — it is exposure, lens choice, eye line and timing, and it is the difference between a property that looks expensive and one that looks like it is trying.

At the top of the market the gap widens. A guest comparing two $1,200-a-night villas, or a buyer comparing two $2M listings, is reading quality signals long before they read the description. Photography that looks considered is itself evidence that the property is well run.

Common questions

Before you book a shoot

How much does real estate photography cost in Costa Rica?

Photography is quoted per property rather than per hour, because a two-bedroom condo and a six-bedroom estate are not the same job. The drivers are the number of rooms, whether you want a twilight exterior, whether drone aerials are included, and travel. Ask for the number before the shoot; ours is agreed in writing first.

Do you shoot luxury villas and estates?

It is most of what we do. Large properties need a different approach to a condo: more time, careful sequencing so the light is right room by room, twilight for the exterior, and aerials to show the setting and the land. An estate is usually a full day on site rather than a couple of hours.

What is a twilight shoot and is it worth it?

It is the twenty to thirty minutes after sunset when the sky still holds colour and the interior lights read warm against it. For a luxury villa or a hotel it produces the single strongest image in the set, and it is almost always the frame that ends up as the listing cover or the homepage hero.

Do you photograph hotels and boutique properties?

Yes, and the brief is different again. A hotel needs rooms shot consistently so a category reads the same across every unit, plus the restaurant, the pool, the spa and the arrival. We also handle Google Street View publication so the property appears inside its own Google listing.

How many photos do I get, and how fast?

Between 25 and 40 edited images for a home or rental, and 60 or more for an estate or a hotel. Delivery is three to five working days, or next day if a listing has a launch date. You get the full edited set, not a selection you have to pay again to unlock.

Can I use the photos on Airbnb, Vrbo, the MLS and my agency site?

Yes. Full usage rights for listing platforms, your own website, social media and paid advertising, with no per-platform licence and no expiry. If you are an agency shooting regularly, we can put that in a standing agreement.

Do you fly drones, and is it legal here?

Yes. Aerials matter more in Costa Rica than almost anywhere, because the setting is often the thing being sold: the ridge, the jungle, the distance to the beach. We fly within Costa Rican civil aviation rules and keep clear of national park airspace and controlled zones without clearance.

Which parts of Costa Rica do you cover?

We are based in La Fortuna and shoot nationwide. Guanacaste, Tamarindo, Papagayo, Nosara, Santa Teresa, Manuel Antonio, the Osa Peninsula, the Central Valley and San Jose are all regular. Travel beyond the Arenal area is quoted as a visible line item rather than hidden in the rate.

Do you do virtual staging or photo editing of furniture?

We colour-correct, straighten verticals, balance windows and clean up minor blemishes. We do not add furniture that is not there or remove permanent features, because a guest or buyer who arrives to something different leaves the review that costs you more than the shoot did.

Should I get stills, a 360 tour, or both?

Stills sell the property on a search grid; a 360 tour answers layout questions once someone is interested. A condo or a simple rental usually needs good stills only. A villa, an estate, a multi-building property or a hotel is where a tour earns its cost, and where the two together do noticeably better than either alone.
