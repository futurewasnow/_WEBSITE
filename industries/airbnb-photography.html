Why it matters

The photos are the product page

A guest scrolling Airbnb gives a listing a couple of seconds. They are not reading the description yet — they are looking at the cover photo and deciding whether to open it at all. Everything after that, the price, the reviews, the amenities, only gets read if the photography earned the click.

Phone photos fail in specific, fixable ways. Interiors come out yellow under warm bulbs. Windows blow out to white so the view you are actually selling disappears. Wide shots bow the walls. Rooms look smaller than they are because the camera was held at standing height instead of chest height. None of that is a taste problem — it is exposure, lens and eye line, and it is what a proper shoot fixes.

In Costa Rica there is a second problem. Most guests are booking from abroad, often for a trip they have been planning for a year, and they cannot drive past to check. The photography has to carry the setting as well as the rooms: what the property looks from the air, what you see from the terrace, how far the beach or the volcano really is.

Common questions

Before you book a shoot

How much does Airbnb photography cost in Costa Rica?

A standard listing shoot starts at 350 USD for a one or two bedroom property and covers the full set of stills a listing needs. Adding a 360 virtual tour or drone aerials moves it to roughly 650 USD. Larger villas and estates are quoted on the number of rooms.

How many photos do I get?

Between 25 and 40 edited stills for a typical property: every bedroom and bathroom, the kitchen, the living space, the outdoor areas and the view. You get the full set, not a selection you have to pay to unlock.

How long does a shoot take?

Two to four hours on site for most rentals, and a full day for a large villa. Editing takes three to five working days. If your listing goes live on a set date, say so when booking and we will work to it.

Do I need the place staged first?

Yes, and it is the single thing that most affects the result. Beds made, surfaces clear, towels fresh, lights working. We will adjust and style small details on the day, but we do not deep-clean or furnish.

What time of day do you shoot?

Interiors in the morning or late afternoon when the light is soft and the windows are not blown out, and exteriors near sunset if the property has a view worth it. In Costa Rica that usually means starting early to work before the afternoon cloud.

Is a 360 virtual tour worth it for a short-term rental?

It depends on the property. For a studio or a simple apartment, good stills are enough. For a villa, a multi-building property or anywhere the layout is hard to read from photos, a tour answers the questions guests would otherwise message you about.

Which parts of Costa Rica do you cover?

We are based in La Fortuna and shoot across Arenal, Guanacaste, Tamarindo, Nosara, Santa Teresa, the Osa Peninsula, the Central Valley and San Jose. Travel outside that is quoted per project.

Can I use the photos on Airbnb, Vrbo and my own site?

Yes. You get full usage rights for listing platforms, your own website, social media and paid ads. There is no per-platform licence and no expiry.
