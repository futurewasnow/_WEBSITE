How WebAR Portals Transform Tours

WebAR brings physical 3D portals into the real world. Visitors drop a doorway into their living room, office, or patio, then physically walk right through.

1. Instant Camera Access

Users tap a link or scan a QR code. Your WebAR experience launches directly in Safari or Chrome without downloading any app.

2. Surface Floor Detection

Advanced SLAM surface tracking detects the ground plane in real time, displaying a targeting reticle to anchor your 3D portal doorway.

3. Step Through the Archway

Walk towards the glowing 3D portal in 6DOF spatial freedom. As you pass through the doorway threshold, you enter the 360° virtual tour environment.

4. Full Gyroscope Exploration

Turn around 360 degrees using your phone’s internal gyroscope sensors to inspect high-resolution HDR details, amenities, and room layouts.
